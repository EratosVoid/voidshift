# Contributing

Thanks for helping out.

1. Open an issue first for anything bigger than a small fix.
2. `pnpm install`, make your change, `pnpm typecheck && pnpm test`.
3. Keep pull requests small and focused on one thing.

## Sign-off

Every commit needs a `Signed-off-by` line (`git commit -s`), certifying the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/): you
wrote the change or have the right to submit it under the Apache-2.0 license.
